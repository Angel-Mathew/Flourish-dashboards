

Dashboards and data stories I built using Flourish.

## 1. Global Tourism Dashboard



A six-slide Flourish story about global tourism. It covers regional arrivals, top revenue countries and the monthly pattern of arrivals and revenue from January 2024 to April 2025.

### The problem
Tourism numbers are large and hard to picture. I wanted to show who visits, who earns the most and how demand changes during the year.

### What you can explore
- Share of tourist arrivals by region (donut chart)
- Top countries by tourism revenue (bar chart, in USD billions)
- Monthly tourist arrivals versus tourism revenue (line chart)

### What I found
- Europe is the largest region by arrivals, well ahead of Asia and North America.
- The United States earns the most tourism revenue (215 billion USD), far ahead of Spain (92), France (68), Italy (56) and Thailand (52).
- Tourism is seasonal. Arrivals climb through spring to a summer peak of about 110 million in July 2024, fall into November, have a smaller bump in December, and reach a low in January 2025. Revenue follows the same shape.



## 2. E-commerce Dashboard



A seven-slide Flourish story about online shopping. It looks at what people buy, when they buy it, how they pay and what device they use.

### The problem
Online shops collect a huge amount of data, but a table of rows does not tell you much. I wanted to turn it into interactive charts, so you can see what sells, when it sells and how customers shop.

### What the slides show
- **Monthly sales:** sales per month for each product category, including Electronics, Toys, Health, Books, Sports, Clothing and Home & Kitchen.
- **Popular product categories:** a pie chart with a dropdown to pick a category, which shows how a product's sales are split across dates.
- **International sale report:** a dot chart of sales by customer and date.
- **Preferred payment methods:** a treemap of the online shopping behaviour dataset, grouped by price range (1000-5000, 5000-10000 and 10000+).
- **Most used device for shopping:** a line chart of return rate (%), with a dropdown to switch device. Hovering shows the payment method and the most purchased category.
